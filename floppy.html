Floppy Disk

Project by: Torben Krahnstoever and Ava Williams

Home Solid State Floppy Disk Optical Tape Hard Disk Drive
Use case A removable physical storage medium made from a thin plastic disk, enclosed in a hard protective casing. Historically was used to save documents, programs, and transfer files between computers. Today, it’s obsolete for modern storage and mostly used for miscellaneous purposes. Examples of this include vintage technology restoration, and various art projects.
Performance For an average 3.5-inch HD floppy disk.

Data transfer rate is 30 KB/s (kilobits per second) to 125 KB/s
Seek time: 80 ms to 100 ms
Rotational Speed: 300 RPM (Revolutions per minute)
Storage capacity: 1.44 MB
Data Longevity Lasts around 10-30 years, depending on storage conditions and disk quality. Data can deteriorate over time.
How data is stored Data is stored as tiny, magnetized particles on the coated disk inside. A read/write head magnetizes small areas of the disk to store information.
